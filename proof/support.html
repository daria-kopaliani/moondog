Proof: Sourdough Calculator — Support

What Proof does

Proof is a sourdough calculator and bake log. Set your baker’s percentages — hydration, salt, levain — and Proof gives you the recipe in grams, including true hydration (the levain counted). Proof Pro adds Hamelman’s dough-temperature method, a levain builder, and a bake log.

Common questions

How do I log my results? Open the Recipe calculator, set your percentages, and read the grams. Log a bake from the Bake log with its formula, rating, and notes.

Where do the ranges come from? Every formula names its source (King Arthur Baking’s baker’s-math convention; Hamelman’s Bread; Robertson’s Tartine Bread). See “Baker’s math” in the app.

Is my data private? Yes — everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

What does the purchase unlock? The Recipe calculator and Baker’s math are free forever. A one-time $6.99 purchase unlocks Dough temperature, the Levain builder, and the Bake log — no subscription.

I paid before and reinstalled. Open Settings → “Restore purchases.”

Important

Proof is an educational reference of published targets — not professional baking instruction. Your flour, starter, and kitchen get the final vote.
