Proof: Sourdough Calculator — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-16

The short version

Proof does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every value you enter (your lab results, dates, and notes) stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, health data, or usage data.

What we store on your device

The app saves the readings and preferences you enter locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Stays on your iPhone or iPad
  • Is never transmitted off your device
  • Is included in your iCloud device backup if you have iCloud backup enabled, per Apple's standard backup behavior
  • Is deleted when you delete the app

In-app purchases

Proof’s recipe calculator is free forever. A single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ($6.99) unlocks the remaining screens permanently — there are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment method, name, address, or any other purchase detail.

Network use

The app does not make any network calls. There are no servers we operate, no analytics endpoints, and no third-party SDKs that contact remote services. Your recipes and bake log never leave the app.

Health data

Proof integrates with no external platform. The formulas and bakes you enter in Proof stay in Proof, on your device.

Not a medical device

Proof is an educational reference and a personal log. It computes baker’s-percentage recipes from values you enter and displays published, named-source baking references (King Arthur Baking’s baker’s-math convention; Jeffrey Hamelman’s Bread; Chad Robertson’s Tartine Bread) for educational purposes only.
