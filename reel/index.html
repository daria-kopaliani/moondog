What it does

Reel answers the extension-cord questions. The Cord Gauge Guide and Cord Safe are free forever: 17 common appliances and tools with their typical amp draws and the minimum gauge at 25, 50, and 100 ft — built on ESFI's published table and the NEC's voltage-drop note, whichever is stricter. Enter the gauge and length of a cord you own and get honest verdicts across the whole catalog.

It also carries the "heavy duty" truth: the words on cord packaging have no standard behind them — the spec is gauge × length × amps, and the gauge is printed on the cord jacket itself. And some answers are permanent: space heaters (per USFA and NFPA) and EV charging (per every EV maker's manual) are never on a cord — no input overrides those rows, and they're free.
