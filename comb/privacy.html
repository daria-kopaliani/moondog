Comb: Varroa Count & Treat — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-16

The short version

Comb does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every value you enter — mite counts, hives, dates, notes — stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, health data, or usage data.

What we store on your device

The app saves the hives, counts and preferences you enter locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). This data:

  • Stays on your iPhone or iPad
  • Is never transmitted off your device
  • Is included in your iCloud device backup if you have iCloud backup enabled, per Apple's standard backup behavior
  • Is deleted when you delete the app

In-app purchases

Comb’s Mite check and Treat safely screens are free forever. A single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ($6.99) unlocks the remaining screens permanently — there are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment method, name, address, or any other purchase detail.

Network use

The app does not make any network calls. There are no servers we operate, no analytics endpoints, and no third-party SDKs that contact remote services. Your hive records never leave the app.

Apiary data

Comb integrates with no external platform. The counts and hive records you enter in Comb stay in Comb, on your device.

Not a pesticide label

Comb is an educational reference and a personal log. It performs arithmetic on values you enter and displays published, named-source guidance (Honey Bee Health Coalition, Tools for Varroa Management, Ninth Edition, 11 June 2026) for educational purposes only. It never states a dose. The product label is the legal authority for application, and registrations differ by state and province.
