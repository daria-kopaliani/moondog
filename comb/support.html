What Comb does

Comb turns a mite wash into a decision. Enter the mites you counted and the size of your sample, and it gives you mites per 100 bees judged against the trigger for the colony phase you are in. Comb Pro adds a treatment finder that sorts every registered control into what fits today's conditions and what does not, plus a hive log and a season plan.

Common questions

Why does my threshold look lower than I remember? Because it is. The Honey Bee Health Coalition published the Ninth Edition of Tools for Varroa Management on 11 June 2026 and tightened Table 1 against the 2022 edition — the population-increase trigger dropped from over 2–3% to 1%, and peak population from over 3% to 2%. Comb shows the edition and date on every verdict.

Why is a product ruled out? Comb shows the one condition that ruled it out — supers on, a temperature outside the label window, colony size, brood state — because those are different problems with different fixes.

Why won't it tell me a dose? The label is the law. Registrations differ by state and province and label text changes between seasons, so Comb does the arithmetic and the calendar and leaves the dose to the label in your hand.

Is my data private? Yes — everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
