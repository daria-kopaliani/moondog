What it does

Loft answers the attic questions. The R-Value Check and Insulate Safe are free forever: pick your climate zone, measure the inches actually up there today (blown insulation settles — Loft asks for today's number), and get an honest estimate against the Energy Star cost-effective target, R30 to R60 by zone. It also carries the chart truth: per the FTC R-value Rule, every package of insulation must state its coverage — R-value marketing earned itself a federal rule. Loft's numbers are planning norms and say so; the chart on the bag outranks any calculator, including this one.
