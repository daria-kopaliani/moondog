Haunch: Raw Dog Food Calc — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-21

The short version

Haunch does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every value you enter — dogs, weights, meal plans, notes — stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, health data, or usage data.

What we store on your device

The app saves the dogs, weights and preferences you enter locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). This data:

  • Stays on your iPhone or iPad
  • Is never transmitted off your device
  • Is included in your iCloud device backup if you have iCloud backup enabled, per Apple's standard backup behavior
  • Is deleted when you delete the app

In-app purchases

Haunch’s Ration, Bone and Feed safely screens are free forever. A single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ($6.99) unlocks the remaining screens permanently — there are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment method, name, address, or any other purchase detail.

Network use

The app does not make any network calls. There are no servers we operate, no analytics endpoints, and no third-party SDKs that contact remote services. Your dogs and their records never leave the app.

Not a product specification

Haunch is an educational reference and a personal log. It performs arithmetic on values you enter and displays published, named-source figures — the feeding percentages and ratios of Raw Dogs Dorset, the edible-bone content figures of Perfectly Rawsome, and the calcium:phosphorus band and large-breed calcium ceiling of the AAFCO Dog Food Nutrient Profiles — for educational purposes only. It is not veterinary advice, it does not diagnose, and it does not certify any diet as complete and balanced. Published guidance differs between sources and changes over time; where the app disagrees with your veterinarian, follow your veterinarian.
