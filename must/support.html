Frequently asked

What's free and what's in Must Pro?

Free forever: Must Check (honey ⇄ gravity ⇄ ABV with BJCP classification), the Nutrient Plan (the full TOSNA schedule at full depth), and Ferment Right. Must Pro ($6.99, one-time) unlocks the Batch Log, Stabilize & Sweeten, and the Style Guide. No subscription.

Where do Must's numbers come from?

Every screen names its source. BJCP strength and sweetness bands are quoted from the 2015 Mead Style Guidelines. The nutrient schedule is computed from Sergio Moutela's published TOSNA formula (Mead Made Right) — total Fermaid-O = ((Brix × 10) × nitrogen factor ÷ 50) × gallons, in four equal doses at 24, 48, and 72 hours plus the ⅓ sugar break or day 7, whichever comes first. Honey-to-gravity uses the published planning figure of ~35 points per pound per gallon (33–37 by variety). Where no authority owns a number, Must says "common practice" out loud.

Which nitrogen demand do I pick for my yeast?

Your strain's maker publishes it — Lallemand and Scott Labs list nitrogen demand for their strains. Pick Low (0.75), Medium (0.90), or High (1.25) to match; the factors are Moutela's. A verified per-strain preset table is on the roadmap.

I missed a nutrient addition — do I double the next one?

No. Per the TOSNA protocol, the final addition lands at the ⅓ sugar break or day 7 — whichever comes first — and nothing gets added after the break. A missed dose is skipped, not stacked; the Batch Log closes un-taken doses once your readings cross the break.

Can I just add sorbate and backsweeten?

Not safely. Two things the app will keep telling you: sorbate cannot stop an active fermentation (it only prevents a finished one from restarting), and the order is sulfite first, then sorbate about 12 hours later — reversed, you risk a geranium off-flavor nothing removes. Ferment out or cold-crash, stabilize in order, then sweeten.

Does Must sell alcohol or ingredients?

No. Must is planning math and a private log for a homebrew hobbyist's own batches — it sells nothing, links to no store, and never leaves your device.
