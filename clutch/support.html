Clutch: Backyard Chicken Log — Support

What Clutch does

Clutch is a backyard-chicken calculator and egg log. Enter how many birds you keep and how old they are, and Clutch gives you the feed stage, the published protein band, how much feed that means and when to switch. Clutch Pro adds flock records, a coop space and daylight check, and an egg log that reports your laying rate per hen.

Common questions

How do I log my results? Open the Feed planner, enter your bird count and their age, and read the result. Log the morning’s eggs from the Egg log — one number a day is the whole habit.

Where do the ranges come from? Every figure names its source (cooperative-extension small-flock nutrition and housing publications; USDA FSIS and extension egg-handling guidance). See “Egg handling” in the app.

Is my data private? Yes — everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

What does the purchase unlock? The Feed planner and Egg handling are free forever. A one-time $6.99 purchase unlocks Flock, Space & light, and the Egg log — no subscription.

I paid before and reinstalled. Open Settings → “Restore purchases.”

Important

Clutch is an educational reference of published targets — not veterinary advice. Your birds, your climate and your vet get the final vote.
