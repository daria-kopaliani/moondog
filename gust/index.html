What it does

Gust answers the air-compressor questions. The Tool CFM Guide and Air Safe are free forever: 16 common air tools with their typical CFM @ 90 psi, and honest verdicts against the compressor you already own — runs, check the plate, bursts only, or undersized. Continuous tools (sanders, grinders, spray guns) are never sugar-coated: they live on pump CFM, and a bigger tank only adds seconds.

It also carries the HP truth: in 2004 the big compressor makers agreed to stop advertising "peak horsepower" — Campbell Hausfeld, DeVilbiss, Ingersoll-Rand, Coleman Powermate. Gust sizes by the number that survived: deliverable CFM @ 90 psi. Air Safe covers OSHA's 30 psi cleaning rule, tank draining (water inside is how receivers rust and rupture), and why a tank is never welded or patched.
