Privacy notice.

This notice applies to the app release service enquiry. Updated November 1, 2026.

Before you send

The enquiry form processes its fields in your browser. It prepares an email draft or a text download.

It does not send the form to a server. It does not save the form in browser storage. Reloading the page can clear your entries.

This service page does not load advertising or analytics scripts. The website host can process ordinary request logs.

When you email

Your email provider and our email provider process the message. We use your contact and project details to assess and deliver the requested service.

We do not add enquiries to a marketing mailing list. Do not send secrets, signing files, payment card details, or customer data.

Project access

We agree on access and confidentiality before receiving private source code. Use sample data when a problem requires a demonstration.

We remove local source copies within 30 days after closure unless the booking summary requires a shorter period.

We retain necessary communication and billing records for service administration and applicable legal requirements.

Questions or requests

Email hello@moondogapps.com for access, correction, or deletion requests. Some billing records may require continued retention.
