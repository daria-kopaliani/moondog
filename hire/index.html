BEFORE YOU START

Clear boundaries. Useful answers.

What if you cannot reproduce the problem?

The report states the attempts, evidence, limitations, and next checks. The diagnosis pays for investigation, not a guaranteed cause or repair.

Do you need my Apple password?

No. Use project access and appropriate App Store Connect invitations when needed. Keep your passwords and signing secrets private.

Can you work with an agency?

Yes. The agency remains the client contact. Agree on confidentiality and delivery ownership in the written scope before work starts.

How does payment work?

We confirm fit and send a booking summary and Stripe invoice. Diagnosis is prepaid. Repair uses a 50% deposit and 50% after acceptance.

Is App Review approval included?

No. We can prepare a corrected build and submission notes. Apple controls its review decisions and timing.
