SCOPE, PAYMENT & HANDOFF

Service details.

These details describe the package. A booking summary identifies the seller, customer, exact scope, price, and start date.

An enquiry does not create a paid booking. Work starts only after both sides accept the booking summary and payment arrives.

Diagnosis

The diagnosis costs USD 250 before any applicable tax. It covers one reported problem in one existing SwiftUI iOS app.

It includes up to two hours of investigation and a written report. The report records findings, evidence, limits, and suggested next steps.

The target delivery is two business days after the agreed start, payment, and required access. The booking summary confirms the dates.

A diagnosis can find that more investigation is necessary. It does not guarantee reproduction, a confirmed cause, or an available repair.

Repair

Repair is optional and separate. We quote USD 750–1,500 for work that fits the package.

The diagnosis fee is not a repair deposit or credit. The combined price is normally USD 1,000–1,750 before any applicable tax.

The quote specifies one root cause, affected components, acceptance checks, exclusions, and delivery dates. Changes require a written scope agreement.

Repair payment is 50% before work starts and 50% after the agreed acceptance checks pass. We do not make unapproved additional charges.

The target delivery is three to five business days after the agreed start and access. Client delays can move the dates.

Acceptance and corrections

You receive the patch and evidence for the agreed checks. Review them within five business days.

Report a failed acceptance check with reproduction steps. The package includes one correction round for failures in the agreed scope.

Silence does not mean acceptance. We agree on unresolved findings before closing the engagement.

For 14 calendar days after acceptance, report a reproducible defect caused by the delivered change. We correct defects within the original scope.

New features, new Apple requirements, unrelated defects, and later client changes require a separate quote.

Cancellation and refunds

If you cancel before work starts, we refund the prepaid amount. If we cannot start by the agreed date, you can cancel.

After diagnosis starts, we complete the report for the diagnosis fee unless both sides agree otherwise.

If repair stops, we document completed work and agree on a fair adjustment against the written scope. We refund any agreed unearned amount.

These terms do not remove rights that applicable law gives you.

Accounts and access

You keep ownership of your code and Apple account. Use repository invitations and appropriate App Store Connect access when necessary.

Do not email passwords, API keys, signing keys, private customer data, or payment card details. We agree on the minimum access required.

You control merging, signing, submission, and release. An individual Apple membership can limit which signing tasks another person can perform.

Apple explains App Store Connect roles and developer team access.

Ownership and confidentiality

The booking summary records ownership of the delivered changes and any pre-existing tools or third-party components.

We do not publish your code, screenshots, or project details without your written permission. We agree on confidentiality terms before receiving private source code.

We remove local source copies within 30 days after closure unless the booking summary requires a shorter period. Billing records follow applicable retention requirements.

Limits

This package does not include a new app, redesign, backend migration, account reinstatement, or policy appeal.

Apple controls review decisions and timing. We do not promise approval, ranking, downloads, or revenue.
