Baste: Quilt Backing & Binding — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-21

The short version

Baste does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every value you enter — quilt measurements, fabric purchases, notes — stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, health data, or usage data.

What we store on your device

The app saves the quilts, fabric purchases and preferences you enter locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). This data:

  • Stays on your iPhone or iPad
  • Is never transmitted off your device
  • Is included in your iCloud device backup if you have iCloud backup enabled, per Apple's standard backup behavior
  • Is deleted when you delete the app

In-app purchases

Baste’s Backing, Binding and Overage screens are free forever. A single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ($6.99) unlocks the remaining screens permanently — there are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment method, name, address, or any other purchase detail.

Network use

The app does not make any network calls. There are no servers we operate, no analytics endpoints, and no third-party SDKs that contact remote services. Your quilts and fabric records never leave the app.

Not a product specification

Baste is an educational reference and a personal log. It performs arithmetic on values you enter and displays published, named-source figures — the backing-overage and binding guidance of The Crafty Quilter, Trace Creek Quilting and Nebraska Quilt Company, and Hobbs Batting's published packaged sizes — for educational purposes only. It does not set standards. Published guidance differs between sources and changes over time, and where the app disagrees with the person who will quilt your quilt, follow your quilter. Where sources conflict, the app shows the conflict rather than resolving it.

Trademarks

The Crafty Quilter, Trace Creek Quilting, Nebraska Quilt Company and Hobbs Batting are trademarks of their respective owners. Baste is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them. Their names appear solely to attribute published figures to the source they came from.
