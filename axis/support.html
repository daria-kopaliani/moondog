Axis: TRT & Hormone Lab Log — Support

What Axis does

Axis is a private log for your hormone labs. Enter your results — total and free testosterone, estradiol, SHBG, LH, FSH, prolactin, hematocrit, PSA, and TSH — and Axis charts each value over time and shows it against a cited clinical reference range.

Common questions

How do I log my results? Tap “Log a draw,” pick the date, and enter the values you have — leave the rest blank. You can also add a single value from any marker's screen.

Where do the ranges come from? Every range names its source (Travison 2017 CDC-harmonized, AUA 2018, Endocrine Society 2018, Jasuja 2023, NORIP, published laboratory intervals). See “Sources & methodology” in Settings.

Is my data private? Yes — everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

What does the purchase unlock? The 48-hour trial lets you try everything. A one-time $9.99 purchase unlocks logging new draws forever — no subscription.

I paid before and reinstalled. Open Settings → “Restore purchases.”

Important

Axis is an educational reference of published targets — not medical advice, and it does not diagnose. Always discuss your results with your physician.
