Loam: Soil Test & Fertilizer

Your lab already told you what the bed needs. Loam turns that into pounds of the bag you bought.

What it does

A soil test report speaks in pounds of nutrient per 1,000 square feet. A fertilizer bag speaks in percentages. Loam is the conversion between them, and it does not pretend to be an agronomist: your own lab's recommendation wins — Loam converts what the report already says into what your bed and your bag need, it does not set rates.

The bag-rate calculator is free forever at full citation quality: enter the recommendation, your bag's analysis and your area, and get pounds and ounces of that specific bag per nutrient — plus an explicit reconciliation when one product cannot hit all three targets without over-delivering one of them.

A one-time $6.99 Loam Pro purchase unlocks lime and sulfur rates (from a printed rate, a buffer pH, or soil texture), per-bed tracking, an application log, organic amendments at first-season availability, and raised-bed fill volumes. No subscription, no ads, no account, works offline.

Sources are named beside the numbers: NMSU Extension Guide H-119, the Adams-Evans and Moore-Sikora buffer methods via the Universities of Delaware and Kentucky, UMass Amherst's Soil & Plant Nutrient Testing Laboratory, University of Missouri MP0733, NC Department of Agriculture Agronomic Services, and the University of Minnesota Soil Testing Laboratory.
