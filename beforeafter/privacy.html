Before & After: Face Morph — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-09

The short version

Before & After does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, usage data — or your photos.

Your photos

Face alignment and morphing run locally on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. The app:

  • Reads only the two photos you explicitly pick (or take in-app)
  • Processes them in memory — it keeps no photo library of its own
  • Never transmits a photo anywhere, for any purpose
  • Saves an export to your photo library only when you tap Save, using add-only access

The camera is used solely to take the before/after photos you ask for. No face data is stored, no face recognition is performed — alignment uses momentary face-landmark geometry that is discarded as soon as the aligned image is produced.

In-app purchases

Before & After offers a one-time Pro purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not see your Apple ID, payment method, or billing information — Apple handles all of that under their own privacy policy. The app only learns whether a successful purchase is associated with the active Apple ID on this device.

Network access

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity is Apple's StoreKit, contacted to fetch the Pro price and to restore purchases.

Data deletion

Delete the app and everything it kept (a free-export counter and your purchase state cache) is removed. Exports you saved to your photo library are yours and remain in Photos until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
