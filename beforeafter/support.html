Common questions

The app says it couldn't detect a face.

Alignment needs a clearly visible, well-lit face in both photos. Front-facing, unobstructed shots work best; heavy shadows, sunglasses, extreme angles, or a face that's very small in the frame can defeat detection. Pick the photos again with clearer shots — nothing is consumed by a failed attempt.

How do the 3 free exports work?

Comparing photos is free forever, with no limit. Exporting is free for your first 3 GIFs (each carries a small corner watermark). After that, a one-time $4.99 Pro purchase unlocks unlimited exports, removes the watermark, and adds full-resolution video and side-by-side formats. There is no subscription.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I restore?

Open the paywall (crown button on the first screen, or any locked row in the share menu) and tap "Restore purchases". Your unlock is restored from the App Store account that originally purchased it.

Why does the second camera shot show a faint overlay?

That's the ghost guide — your "before" photo at low opacity over the live preview, so you can match framing and distance for the "after" shot. It makes the automatic alignment even better, but it's optional; any two photos of the same face will align.

What does the Mirror button do?

Front-camera photos are stored un-mirrored (the way others see you), which can look subtly "off" to you. Mirror flips both photos so they match what you see in a mirror. It affects exports too — pick whichever version reads right to you.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Alignment, morphing, and every export run entirely on your device. The only network activity is Apple's StoreKit for the Pro price and purchase restore.

Where are my photos stored?

The app keeps no photo library of its own. It reads the two photos you pick, processes them in memory, and saves an export to your Photos only when you tap Save. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. The only things it kept — a free-export counter and purchase state — are removed with it.
