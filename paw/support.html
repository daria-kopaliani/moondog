Paw: Pet Bloodwork Log — Support

What Paw does

Paw is a private bloodwork log for dogs and cats. Create a profile per pet, enter the values from each lab panel — creatinine, SDMA, BUN, phosphorus, ALT, ALP, glucose, albumin, total protein, hematocrit — and Paw charts each value over time against a cited, species-specific veterinary reference range.

Common questions

How do I log my results? Tap “Log bloodwork,” pick the date, and enter the values from the lab report — leave the rest blank. You can also add a single value from any marker’s screen.

Where do the ranges come from? Every range names its source (the Merck Veterinary Manual reference tables; the IRIS SDMA limit), per species. See “Sources & methodology” in Settings.

Is my data private? Yes — everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

What does the purchase unlock? The 48-hour trial lets you try everything. A one-time $9.99 purchase unlocks unlimited pets and bloodwork logging forever — no subscription.

I paid before and reinstalled. Open Settings → “Restore purchases.”

Important

Paw is an educational reference of published targets — not veterinary advice, and it does not diagnose. Always review your pet’s results with your veterinarian.
