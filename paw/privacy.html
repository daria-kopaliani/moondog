Paw: Pet Bloodwork Log — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-15

The short version

Paw does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every value you enter (your lab results, dates, and notes) stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, health data, or usage data.

What we store on your device

The app saves the readings and preferences you enter locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Stays on your iPhone or iPad
  • Is never transmitted off your device
  • Is included in your iCloud device backup if you have iCloud backup enabled, per Apple's standard backup behavior
  • Is deleted when you delete the app

In-app purchases

Paw is free to download with a 48-hour trial. A single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ($9.99) unlocks the app permanently — there are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment method, name, address, or any other purchase detail.

Network use

The app does not make any network calls. There are no servers we operate, no analytics endpoints, and no third-party SDKs that contact remote services. Your pet’s lab values never leave the app.

Health data

Paw does not integrate with HealthKit or any health platform. The lab values you enter in Paw stay in Paw, on your device.

Not a medical device

Paw is an educational reference and a personal log. It does not measure, diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. It displays values you enter alongside published, named-source veterinary reference ranges (the Merck Veterinary Manual reference tables and the IRIS SDMA reference limit, per species) for educational purposes only. These are general published ranges, not personalized goals, and it concerns animal health only. Always review your pet’s results with your veterinarian.
