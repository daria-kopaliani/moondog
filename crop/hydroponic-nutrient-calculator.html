FAQ

How do I convert EC to ppm?
Multiply EC (mS/cm) by 500, 640 or 700 depending on your meter's scale. EC 1.4 is 700 ppm on the 500 scale or 980 ppm on the 700. The same solution shows different ppm on different meters — the EC is the fixed value.

What ppm should my nutrients be?
Crop- and stage-dependent. Leafy greens run lean, fruiting crops higher. Use lookup 3 for the per-element figures; the EC band there is the fastest daily check.

Why doesn't the label's NPK match what I'm dosing?
Because the P and K on a label are oxides. Converter 2 turns K₂O and P₂O₅ into the real elemental K and P — a 4-18-38 is closer to 4-7.9-31.5 elemental.
