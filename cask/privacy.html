Cask: Winemaking & SO2 Log — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-16

The short version

Cask does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every value you enter — pH readings, gravity readings, additions, batches, notes — stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, health data, or usage data.

What we store on your device

The app saves the batches, readings and preferences you enter locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). This data:

  • Stays on your iPhone or iPad
  • Is never transmitted off your device
  • Is included in your iCloud device backup if you have iCloud backup enabled, per Apple's standard backup behavior
  • Is deleted when you delete the app

In-app purchases

Cask’s Campden check and Additions safety screens are free forever. A single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ($6.99) unlocks the remaining screens permanently — there are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment method, name, address, or any other purchase detail.

Network use

The app does not make any network calls. There are no servers we operate, no analytics endpoints, and no third-party SDKs that contact remote services. Your batch records never leave the app.

Winemaking data

Cask integrates with no external platform. The batches and readings you enter in Cask stay in Cask, on your device.

Not a food-safety authority

Cask is an educational reference and a personal log. It performs published arithmetic on values you enter and displays named-source winemaking references (Australian Wine Research Institute molecular SO₂ tables; TTB limits) for educational purposes only. Sulfite limits and additive rules vary by jurisdiction, and the regulation that applies where you are is the authority.
