What it does

Flame answers the propane questions. The Burn Time calculator, Tank Guide, and Heat Safe are free forever: pick your tank (per AmeriGas, a 20 lb cylinder holds ~4.7 usable gallons ≈ 430,000 BTU at the EIA's 92,000 BTU/gal), enter the appliance's rated BTU/hr, and get hours and days — always framed honestly: the rated figure is the appliance's maximum draw, so the answer is the floor, not a promise.

Heat Safe carries the rules that matter: the soapy-water leak test (never a match), NFPA's caveat that the rotten-egg odorant is not guaranteed, and CPSC's carbon-monoxide rule — outdoor equipment never runs indoors.
