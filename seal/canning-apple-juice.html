How to can apple juice: time by the book

Updated 20264 min read

Apple juice is high-acid, so a boiling-water bath is safe — here are the USDA processing times and how time changes with elevation.

How long do you can apple juice?

USDA processes apple juice for 5 minutes for pints and 5 minutes for quarts, at a full rolling boil, jars covered by at least an inch of water. Ball Test Kitchen (2024) acidified per 6-pint batch. USDA (2015) does not require added acid. Both sources shown.

Altitude adjustment

At altitude a boiling-water bath needs more time: add 5 minutes at 1,001–3,000 ft, 10 minutes at 3,001–6,000 ft, 15 minutes at 6,001–8,000 ft and 20 minutes above 8,000 ft.

Why apple juice is water-bath canned

Apple juice is high-acid (pH 4.6 or below), so a boiling-water bath reaches a safe temperature. Leave the headspace your tested recipe lists and check every lid sealed before storing.
