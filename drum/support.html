Frequently asked

What's free and what's in Drum Pro?

Free forever: Roast Check (the full DTR, weight-loss and phase math), Roast Levels (the SCA colour scale), and Roast Right. Drum Pro ($6.99, one-time) unlocks the Roast Log, Bean Shelf, and Phase Planner. No subscription.

Why does every number have a little badge on it?

Because they are not the same kind of number. The Specialty Coffee Association's Agtron colour classification is a published standard. Development time ratio and the phase structure are conventions from the roasting literature — widely shared, genuinely useful, and not standards. Weight loss is a measurement with typical ranges. Crack temperatures are physical events that your probe reads differently than mine. Most roasting advice blurs all four together; Drum labels them.

My DTR is outside 20–25%. Is that wrong?

Not necessarily. That band is where most roasters land and it is a good default, but it is convention rather than a rule. Plenty of deliberate light roasts sit under it and darker profiles sit over it. Drum tells you where you are and what it usually means in the cup, then leaves the decision to you.

Can Drum measure my roast colour?

No, and neither can any other phone app. Agtron numbers come from a spectrophotometer. Drum references the published scale so you can aim at a level and record what you targeted — it never claims to read colour from a photo.

Does it connect to my roaster or a thermocouple?

Not in this version. Drum is an offline, pay-once log — you enter the times and weights and it does the math. Hardware integration is on the roadmap but deliberately out of v1.

What's the most important thing on the free Roast Right screen?

Chaff burns. The genuine hazard in home roasting is a fire from accumulated chaff, not a bad cup — clean the collector every roast and never leave a machine unattended from charge to cool.
