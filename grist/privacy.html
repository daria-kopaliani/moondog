Grist: Yarn Substitution — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-21

The short version

Grist does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every value you enter — yarn measurements, your stash, your projects, notes — stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, health data, or usage data.

What we store on your device

The app saves the yarns, projects and preferences you enter locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). This data:

  • Stays on your iPhone
  • Is never transmitted off your device
  • Is included in your iCloud device backup if you have iCloud backup enabled, per Apple's standard backup behavior
  • Is deleted when you delete the app

In-app purchases

Grist’s Substitute and Measure it screens are free forever. The remaining screens — How much to buy, Stash and Projects — are open for a seven-day trial, counted in days you actually open the app rather than days on the calendar, after which a single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ($6.99) unlocks them permanently. There are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment method, name, address, or any other purchase detail.

Network use

The app does not make any network calls. There are no servers we operate, no analytics endpoints, and no third-party SDKs that contact remote services. Your yarns and projects never leave the app.

Not a product specification

Grist is an educational reference and a personal log. It performs arithmetic on values you enter and displays published, named-source figures — the Craft Yarn Council's Standard Yarn Weight System, the wraps-per-inch charts of Purple Lamb Fiber Arts and Craftsy, and Handwoven's definition of grist — for educational purposes only. It does not set standards. Published guidance differs between sources and changes over time; where the sources conflict, the app shows the conflict rather than resolving it. Nothing replaces knitting a gauge swatch.

Trademarks
